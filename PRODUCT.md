# Legal Dhara Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Legal Dhara serves Indian founders, business owners, professionals, and creators who need help completing legal registrations, intellectual-property filings, tax work, and compliance processes.

## Product Purpose

The website helps customers understand a legal service, create a verified account, submit an application, provide documents, pay online, and track progress through one customer-facing legal desk.

## Positioning

Legal Dhara combines guided digital intake with access to legal-service support instead of presenting users with an unassisted catalogue of forms.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive with limited legal terminology, compare multiple registration options, and need confidence about requirements, pricing, timelines, document safety, and what happens after payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Customer authentication uses Firebase email/Google identity plus self-hosted phone verification.
- The production API is hosted separately at `api.legaldhara.com`.
- Payments use Razorpay.
- The customer website is a statically exported Next.js application deployed through Cloudflare Workers Static Assets.
- Phase one preserves current routes, factual content, service flows, authentication behavior, and payment behavior.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is “Legal Dhara.”
- The approved identity uses the supplied black-and-gold LD monogram with a “Legal Dhara” wordmark in the primary header.
- The approved visual direction is premium, mature, investment-focused, and close to the supplied logo.
- Gold is a controlled accent rather than a background treatment.

## Product Principles

- Explain legal work in language a customer can act on.
- Make trust visible through clarity, restraint, and predictable next steps.
- Keep primary actions obvious without making every element promotional.
- Preserve speed and accessibility on mobile connections.
- Use one consistent vocabulary across discovery, signup, payment, and status tracking.
